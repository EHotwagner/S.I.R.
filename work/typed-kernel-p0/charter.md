# Typed protocol kernel P0 baseline and pilot charter Charter

## Identity
- Work id: `typed-kernel-p0`
- Lifecycle stage: charter
- Status: chartered

## Principles
- Preserve the authoritative combat corpus and all runtime/wire behavior.
- Measure and freeze evidence before proposing a reusable abstraction.
- Admit a candidate shared concept only when at least two checked uses exist.
- Keep S.I.R. gameplay values, formula semantics, algorithms, and interpreters domain-owned.

## Scope Boundaries
- In scope: a content-addressed P0 pilot manifest, a deterministic capture/check command,
  baseline authoring/coherence measurements, and the candidate ownership cut.
- Out of scope: the P1 specification AST/EDSL, rule migration, package extraction,
  coordination protocol types, provider lifecycle changes, or runtime behavior changes.
- The roadmap in `FS-GG/.github` is design authority; this independently landable
  S.I.R. slice is governed by Standard SDD.

## Acceptance Boundary
- The P0 manifest names checked examples of facts, formulas, transitions, registered
  algorithms, evidence, generated projections, and historical replay binding.
- Predicate and supersession are reported honestly as validation/coherence fixtures when
  they are not live canonical registry nodes; the baseline must not invent authority.
- Every frozen artifact carries a SHA-256 digest and regenerates byte-identically.
- Current .NET/Fable corpus parity remains green and no authoritative rule bytes change.
- The report records authoring questions, revisions, diagnostics, elapsed work, and
  semantic-diff mechanics from the available historical SDD receipts, using `unknown`
  rather than silent estimates when evidence does not exist.
