## §2 What worked

Treating initial delivery as a versioned default-route contract kept the real measurement and failing mutations while avoiding unsafe compression. A small target-filter policy made modified workspace shortcuts and focused native tactical actions coexist, and the complete browser suite caught both sides of that boundary. Presizing the canonical identity stream from its exact UTF-8 grammar removed the remaining per-byte collection growth without changing any identity or cross-runtime byte semantics.

## §3 What did not

Property-mangling experiments reduced bytes but changed browser semantics and were discarded. Late loader restructuring was also rejected from #186 as too invasive and risky because the current feature-loader contract is implicit across Fable, Vite, public messages, and browser controls; follow-up issue #214 now owns canonizing that boundary. The first unmangled republish also exposed a focused-button shortcut regression and a stale broad assertion. After the keyboard repair changed an implementation-identity subject, the standalone rules gate correctly rejected the stale source pin until it was fully rebound.
